About

I am a Ph.D. student in GSAI at POSTECH, working in the Language & Intelligence Lab under the POSTECH NLP Group, advised by Prof. Hyounghun Kim. Also, I am currently a visiting scholar at the Conversational AI Lab @ UIUC, hosted by Prof. Dilek Hakkani-Tür. Previously, I was a research assistant at the Handong Artificial Intelligence Lab (HAIL), where I advised by Prof. Charmgil Hong.
